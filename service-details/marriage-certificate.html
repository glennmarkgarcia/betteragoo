Marriage Certificate (Local Registration)

Registration of Certificate of Marriage at the Local Civil Registrar

Processing

5 Minutes

Fee

Free

Who Can Apply

Contracting Parties

Appointment

Walk-in

Important Registration Deadlines

The Certificate of Marriage (COM) of a Civil or Church wedding with Marriage License must be registered within fifteen (15) days after the solemnization of marriage. COM of a Civil or Church Wedding under Art. 34 or PD 1083 must be registered within thirty (30) days after the solemnization of marriage. Registration of marriage certificate is free of charge, pursuant to Section 6 of P.D. No. 651.

Step-by-Step Process

Follow these steps to register your marriage certificate

1

Submit Certificate of Marriage

Submit the accomplished Certificate of Marriage (COM) for review. The COM must be duly signed by the contracting parties, solemnizing officer, and at least two witnesses.

2

Claim Registered Certificate

Get your copy of COM duly registered and signed by the Receiving Officer and MCR or any authorized signatory. Accomplish Client Feedback Form and drop at designated suggestion box.

Requirements

Documents needed for marriage registration

For Timely Registration (Within 15/30 Days)

  • Certificate of Marriage duly signed by the contracting parties
  • Signature of the solemnizing officer
  • Signatures of at least two (2) witnesses

For Delayed Registration

  • Affidavit for Delayed Registration of Marriage (at the back of the document shall be accomplished)
  • PSA negative certification
  • Affidavit of the solemnizing officer or the person reporting or presenting the marriage certificate for registration stating the facts and circumstances surrounding the marriage and the reason or cause of delay

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee for marriage certificate registration and licensing?
Registration of a timely marriage certificate is free of charge under P.D. 651. A local certified copy costs ₱80 (₱50 transcript + ₱30 DST). For Marriage License applications, total fees are ₱380 (License Fee ₱250, Application Fee ₱100, DST ₱30).
What is required to apply for a Marriage License in Agoo?
Applicants must submit: (1) Birth Certificates or Certified Copies, (2) CENOMAR from PSA, (3) Barangay Clearance, (4) Pre-Marriage Counseling (PMC) Certificate from MSWDO/Population Office, (5) Parental Consent (for ages 18–21) or Advice (for ages 21–25), and (6) 2x2 photos. Counter evaluation takes 40 minutes, followed by a mandatory 10-consecutive-day publication period.
What is the deadline for registering a Marriage Certificate?
For civil or church weddings with a Marriage License: within 15 days after solemnization. For weddings under Article 34 (cohabitation for 5+ years): within 30 days. Timely registration processing takes only 5 minutes at the counter.
Can I request a PSA Marriage Certificate or CENOMAR in Agoo?
Yes. You can apply for PSA Marriage Certificates (₱60 service fee + ₱155 PSA fee) or CENOMAR (₱60 service fee + ₱210 PSA fee) via the BREQS outlet at the Agoo Civil Registrar Office.

Application for Marriage License

For engaged couples intending to get married in Agoo

Important Information

Engaged couples (either one or both of them is a resident of Agoo, NV) of legal ages who are intending to get married must apply for marriage license at the MCRO. The Marriage License is valid anywhere in the Philippines for a period of 120 days from the date of issue.

10 Days Total Processing
₱270.00 Total Fees
