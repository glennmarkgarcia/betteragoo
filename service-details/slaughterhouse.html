Slaughter of Hog

Hog slaughter service with ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection

Requirements

  • Shipping permit / Certificate of Ownership / Certificate of Transfer
  • Veterinary Health Certificate
Step Activity Fee Duration Personnel
1 Present shipping permit / Certificate of Ownership / Certificate of Transfer and Veterinary Health Certificate — 2 minutes Meat Inspector
2 Ante-mortem inspection of hog — 5 minutes Meat Inspector
3 Pay slaughter fee at the Slaughterhouse Office See fee schedule 3 minutes Slaughterhouse Supervisor
4 Slaughter of hog — 30 minutes Butcher
5 Post-mortem inspection — 5 minutes Meat Inspector
6 Issuance of Meat Inspection Certificate — 2 minutes Meat Inspector
Category Weight Range Slaughter Fee
Small Below 50 kg ₱80.00
Medium 50 - 80 kg ₱100.00
Large Above 80 kg ₱120.00

Processing Information

  • Total processing time: 47 minutes
  • Please accomplish Client Feedback Form and drop at the designated suggestion box

Slaughter of Carabao

Carabao slaughter service with ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection

Requirements

  • Shipping permit / Certificate of Ownership / Certificate of Transfer
  • Veterinary Health Certificate
  • Large Cattle Registration Certificate
  • Slaughter permit from the Municipal Agriculturist
Step Activity Fee Duration Personnel
1 Present shipping permit / Certificate of Ownership / Certificate of Transfer, Veterinary Health Certificate, Large Cattle Registration Certificate, and Slaughter permit — 5 minutes Meat Inspector
2 Ante-mortem inspection of carabao — 10 minutes Meat Inspector
3 Pay slaughter fee at the Slaughterhouse Office See fee schedule 3 minutes Slaughterhouse Supervisor
4 Slaughter of carabao — 1 hour Butcher
5 Post-mortem inspection — 10 minutes Meat Inspector
6 Issuance of Meat Inspection Certificate — 2 minutes Meat Inspector
Category Weight Range Slaughter Fee
Small Below 250 kg ₱250.00
Medium 250 - 400 kg ₱300.00
Large Above 400 kg ₱350.00

Processing Information

  • Total processing time: 1 hour 30 minutes
  • Carabao slaughter requires special permit from the Municipal Agriculturist
  • Please accomplish Client Feedback Form and drop at the designated suggestion box

Office Information

Important Notes

  • All animals must pass ante-mortem inspection before slaughter
  • Meat Inspection Certificate is required for meat transport
  • Carabao slaughter requires special permit from Municipal Agriculturist
