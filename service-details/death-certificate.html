Registration of Death Certificate

Registration of Certificate of Death at the Local Civil Registrar

Processing

1 hr 35-38 mins

Fee

₱50-150

Who Can Apply

Informant/Family

Deadline

Within 30 Days

Important Registration Information

The registration of Death Certificate (DC) at the MCRO within thirty (30) days is mandatory. Delayed registration of death must be filed following the lapse of the prescribed period of 30 calendar days from the death of a person. Registration of death certificate is free of charge, pursuant to Section 6 of P.D. No. 651.

Requirements

Documents needed for death certificate registration

For Death in Hospital/Clinic

  • Certificate of Death duly signed by attending physician
  • Valid ID of informant
  • Embalmer's Certification

For Death at Home (Unattended)

  • Information about the deceased (full name, date of birth, date of death, etc.)
  • Valid ID of informant
  • Embalmer's Certification
  • MHO Certification of Cause of Death

For Delayed Registration (After 30 Days)

  • Affidavit for Delayed Registration of Death
  • PSA negative certification
  • Supporting documents (hospital records, burial records, etc.)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee for death certificate registration and permits?
Registration of a timely death certificate (within 30 days) is free of charge under P.D. 651. A local certified copy costs ₱80 (₱50 transcript + ₱30 DST). Permit fees: Burial Permit is ₱50.00, Transfer Permit (for burial outside Agoo) is ₱100.00, and Resident Cemetery Vault fee is ₱7,250.00.
What is the deadline for registering a death certificate in Agoo?
Death certificates must be registered within 30 calendar days from the date of death. Registrations beyond 30 days are processed as Delayed Registration (13-minute counter evaluation) requiring a PSA Negative Certification, Affidavit of Informant, and 2 supporting documents.
What is the difference between a Burial Permit and a Transfer Permit?
A Burial Permit (₱50.00) is required for interment in any public or private cemetery within Agoo. A Transfer Permit (₱100.00) is required if the deceased's remains will be transported outside Agoo to another municipality for burial.
How long does death registration and permit processing take?
For deaths occurring in a hospital or clinic: counter processing takes approximately 1 hour and 38 minutes. For deaths at home: counter processing takes approximately 1 hour and 35 minutes (includes MHO medical review, Treasurer fee payment, and LCR registration).
Can I request a PSA Death Certificate directly at Agoo Municipal Hall?
Yes! LGU Agoo operates a BREQS (Batch Request System) outlet at the Civil Registrar Office. You can request national PSA Death Certificates (₱60 service fee + ₱155 PSA fee) directly without traveling to regional PSA centers.
