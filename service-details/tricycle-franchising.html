Frequently Asked Questions

Charter-verified answers for tricycle franchising and records in Agoo

What is the total fee for an annual Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP)?
The total annual MTOP fee is ₱1,440.00 (Mayor's Permit ₱450, Renewal ₱100, Supervision ₱50, Verification ₱50, Tax Clearance ₱50, Garbage ₱200, Health Exam ₱140, Drug Test ₱250, Business Plate ₱150).
How long is an MTOP Franchise valid and what is the renewal fee?
Tricycle franchises are valid for 3 years. The 3-year franchise validity renewal fee is ₱500.00 (processed via Sangguniang Bayan Committee on Transportation and BPLS).
What are the requirements for MTOP renewal?
Submit Barangay Clearance, Driver's License (Professional), LTO OR/CR, Drug Test result, Medical Certificate, and Police Clearance.
