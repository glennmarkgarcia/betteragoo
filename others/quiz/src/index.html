THE 4 CIVIC RANK TIERS

Rise through the ancient ranks of Agoo! Complete 10 civic trials to earn thy badge and claim thy place in the Hall of Champions.

Eagle Master Ranks #1 – #25
Score: 9 – 10 / 10

The pinnacle of civic mastery! Awarded to supreme scholars who score 9–10/10 with swift precision. Inspired by the iconic MacArthur Highway Eagle Statue watching over Agoo.

Basilica Scholar Ranks #26 – #50
Score: 8 / 10

A guardian of history and heritage! Earned by high achievers (8/10). Named after the historic Basilica Minore of Our Lady of Charity, symbolizing deep roots and wisdom.

Dinengdeng Explorer Ranks #51 – #75
Score: 6 – 7 / 10

A spirited explorer of local culture! Earned by solid trial contenders (6–7/10). Celebrating Agoo's famous annual Dinengdeng Festival and rich agricultural roots.

Agoho Trailblazer Ranks #76 – #100
Score: 0 – 5 / 10

The journey to glory begins here! Earned by promising seekers (0–5/10). Rooted in the resilient Agoho trees along coastal shores that gave our municipality its name.
