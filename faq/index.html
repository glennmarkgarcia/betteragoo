FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about municipal services

General Questions

What are the office hours of the Municipal Hall?

The Municipal Hall is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with a lunch break from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on weekends and national/local holidays.

How can I contact a specific municipal office?

Visit our Government Directory page to find contact information for all municipal offices and department heads.

Can I request services online?

Yes! LGU Agoo has 11 live online service routes powered by eLGU BPLS at elgu-bpls.com for Business Permit billing & renewal, Real Property Tax billing & payment, Public Market rental fees, and Waterworks billing. Walk-in services are available at Municipal Hall, Monday to Friday 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.

Certificates & Vital Records

How long does it take to get a birth certificate?

For birth certificates registered in Agoo, a local certified copy costs ₱80 (₱50 transcript + ₱30 Documentary Stamp Tax) and is released in 5–15 minutes if the record is available.

Can I request PSA birth, marriage, or death certificates in Agoo?

Yes. The Agoo Local Civil Registry Office operates a BREQS (Batch Request System) outlet for PSA documents. Service fee is ₱60 per document, plus PSA fees: ₱155 for Birth/Marriage/Death certificates or ₱210 for CENOMAR.

Can someone else request my certificate for me?

Yes, but they must bring:

  • An authorization letter signed by you
  • Valid government-issued ID of both you and the representative
  • Photocopies of both IDs
What is the difference between PSA and local civil registrar certificates?

Both are official certified true copies. PSA certificates are printed on security paper (SECPA) for national applications (passports, visas, DFA). Local civil registrar certificates (₱80) are issued directly on-site and accepted for local transactions, employment, and school enrollment.

Business, Permits & Licensing

When should I renew my business permit and what are the penalties for late renewal?

Business permits must be renewed annually during January (deadline January 20). Late renewals incur a 25% surcharge plus 2% monthly interest (up to a maximum of 72%) per the Agoo Revenue Code of 2013. Renewal counter processing takes only 5 minutes at the BPLS Office once clearances are complete.

What do I need to start a new business in Agoo?

To apply for a new Business Permit (10-minute counter processing), prepare:

  • DTI Business Name Registration (Sole Proprietorship) or SEC Registration (Corporation/Partnership)
  • Barangay Business Clearance
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) for the current year
  • Zoning / Locational Clearance from MPDO
  • Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from BFP

Applications can also be submitted online via eLGU BPLS BPLS New Business.

How much does a Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) cost in Agoo?

The total annual MTOP fee is ₱1,440 (Mayor's Permit ₱450, Annual Renewal ₱100, Supervision ₱50, Verification ₱50, Tax Clearance ₱50, Garbage Fee ₱200, Health Exam ₱140, Drug Test ₱250, Business Plate ₱150). Tricycle franchise validity renewal (every 3 years) is ₱500.

How do I apply for a Motorized Banca Permit?

Submit your application at the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO). The total annual fee is ₱400 (License fee ₱200, Inspection fee ₱100, Permit Sticker ₱100). Processing takes approximately 30 minutes.

Building & Engineering Permits

What are the fees and processing times for a Building Permit?

The standard Building Permit Application Fee is ₱150, Inspection Fee is ₱500, plus Fire Safety Evaluation Fee (₱100 residential / ₱130 commercial) and structural assessment fees based on floor area. Processing time is 2 hours 30 minutes minimum (up to 5 working days for complex building reviews).

What is required to get a Certificate of Occupancy?

Application Fee is ₱150 and Final Inspection Fee is ₱500. Requirements include Completed Certificate of Completion signed by supervising engineer/architect, As-Built Plans, and Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) for Occupancy from BFP. Minimum processing time is 1 hour 30 minutes.

Payments, Taxes & Fees

What payment methods are accepted?

Over-the-counter payments at the Municipal Treasurer's Office are made in cash. Online payments for Business Permits, Real Property Tax, Market Rental, and Water Bills are accepted 24/7 via debit card, credit card, and e-wallets through the Agoo eLGU BPLS portal.

How can I pay my real property tax (Amilyar) and what is the tax rate?

Real Property Tax in Agoo is 1% Basic Tax + 1% Special Education Fund (SEF) of the assessed property value. You can view your bill and pay online via eLGU BPLS RPT Billing or visit the Municipal Treasurer's Office (10-minute counter processing).

How much does a Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) cost?

For individuals: ₱5.00 base tax + ₱1.00 for every ₱1,000.00 of gross annual income/earnings. For corporations/entities: ₱500.00 base tax + ₱2.00 for every ₱5,000.00 of gross property/earnings. Issuance takes 5 minutes at Window 1, Municipal Hall.

How do I request a Certified Copy of a Tax Declaration or Property Inspection?

Certified True Copies of Tax Declarations cost ₱105.00 per copy at the Municipal Assessor's Office. Ocular Property Inspection for land valuation costs ₱100.00 per lot.

Social Services

How do I apply for a Senior Citizen ID?

Go to the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) with:

  • Birth Certificate or any valid ID showing your age (60 and above)
  • 1x1 ID photo
  • Barangay Residence Certificate

The ID is issued for free.

What benefits do senior citizens receive?

Senior citizens enjoy 20% discount and VAT exemption on purchases (with a minimum purchase amount per establishment), priority lanes, and access to special programs and medical assistance from the municipality.

Who developed Better Agoo?

Glenn Mark P. Garcia is the developer behind Abakada.org and BetterAgoo.org, a community-driven initiative building a digital bridge between the residents of Agoo and the local government of Agoo. Based in the United Arab Emirates, he works in IT and practices full-stack development, helping build practical digital solutions in web development, design, cloud services, and cybersecurity.
